          How to Win
          Rule 1Claim the snow and score
          • Every company rides as a sleigh squad: 4 players, 4 sleighs, one colour, one logo, one shared territory.
          • Teammates can close each other's boxes. One rides out, another rides into their ribbon, and the whole box is claimed for your company. Split up, cut off rivals and box them in together.
          • Ride out from your squad's land and loop back to it, or close a loop on your own ribbon. Everything inside becomes your company's, stamped with your logo.
          • Every empty square you wrap: +1 point.
          • Every square you take from a rival: +5 points.
          • Circle all the way round a rival sleigh: +10 points. That sleigh is out and their land inside your loop becomes yours.
          • Ride into Santa: +10 points. He falls over, gets up and runs off.
          Rule 2Controls, snowballs, Zoom and the map
          • Steer with the arrow keys, or swipe on a phone.
          • S throws a snowball. You earn one for every 10 points. A hit does no damage, but the rival spins round once and loses control. Santa throws snowballs at anyone.
          • Z is Zoom: nitro, triple speed for a moment. You earn a Zoom every time you circle a rival.
          • Hills launch you into a short jump at double speed, over anything in the way.
          • Trees, blocks, the rocket, the van and the alien spaceship stop you dead. Steer to ride on.
          • Two rival sleighs that crash head-on both spin to a stop, then ride on. Teammates ride straight through each other, and can never engulf each other.
          Rule 3Squads, heats and the grand final
          • The only way out is being engulfed by a rival. A sleigh that's engulfed is out. A company is out when all 4 of its sleighs are.
          • An ice wall closes in and the map keeps getting smaller. Hit it and you stop. It pushes sleighs inwards but never knocks anyone out. Land under the ice is lost.
          • Heats: 25 companies per map, about 15 minutes each. The ice wall makes sure every heat ends on time. The last company standing goes through. If more than one is left at the buzzer, the biggest territory goes through.
          • Heat winners meet heat winners, round after round, until the grand final: one big map, live on the homepage for everyone to watch. The game is played once and never again, so the champion's logo stays on the map forever.
          • Scores are kept by our game servers, never by your browser, so everyone sees the same scores. Practice is free and open to everyone now, and never counts on any leaderboard.
